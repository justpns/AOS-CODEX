06 · Implementation notes

Reference: existing EXP Handoff v1.0, original monster rewards and proposed HP. Per-level best theoretical normal monster within ±5 levels; full-buff DPS curve, 2s overhead, 100% uptime, no spawn cap. TTK = HP / effective DPS; kills/hour = 3600 / (TTK + 2); EXP/hour = kills/hour × reward × boost. Boss, quest, party and AoE excluded.

Class 1 transition at Base 50; Class 2 Job 70 target Base 135. Job EXP earned concurrently. Class change resets Job EXP. Tables are proposed, not production-approved. The 24h/day comparison is theoretical. Real DPS, spawn capacity, party sharing and boost stacking require playtest. Use signed 64-bit cumulative EXP. Policy changes scale all three EXP tables together and re-round integer requirements; no monster rewards are changed.
